What makes this "quantum-inspired"?

Each deployment decision (use vessel, speed, cargo split, fuel choice, shore power) is encoded as a qubit-style probability amplitude. Every generation samples a candidate fleet plan from that probability field, evaluates it, then rotates the whole field toward the best-known plan — steering the entire search distribution at once rather than only surviving individuals, as a classical GA does. It runs on ordinary CPUs; no quantum hardware is required or simulated.

This is validated, not just claimed — see the Benchmarking tab for a live, same-objective comparison against a classical GA, random search, and today's greedy planning approach.
